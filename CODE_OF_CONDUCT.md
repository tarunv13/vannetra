# Code of conduct

WildTrace documents wildlife crime. That work attracts strong feelings, and sometimes people with
an interest in what is published. The rules here are short.

**Be decent.** Discuss the evidence, not the person. No harassment, insults, or personal attacks.

**Never name private individuals.** Not in issues, pull requests, commits or discussions. That
includes suspects, sellers, informants and anyone in a photograph or listing, whether or not a
news report already named them. Use case IDs and links to sources instead.

**Bring evidence.** Claims about a case need a source. "This is wrong" without a link cannot be
acted on.

**Respect the law and the sites we collect from.** No scraping that breaks terms of use, no
sharing of restricted datasets.

Breaking these rules gets your contribution closed, and repeated breaches get you blocked from the
repository. Report a problem privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/tarunv13/wildtrace/security/advisories/new) or by
opening an issue if it is safe to do so in public. The maintainer decides and will say what was
decided.
