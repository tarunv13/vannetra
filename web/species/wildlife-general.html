Wildlife (unspecified) in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 405 cases involving wildlife (unspecified) from public reports between 2024-01-15 and 2026-09-26, in 38 countries. 92 of them rest on an official or validated source.

Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.
