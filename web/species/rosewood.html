Rosewood & timber in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 47 cases involving rosewood & timber from public reports between 2024-01-15 and 2026-09-22, in 10 countries. 3 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Dalbergia spp., Pterocarpus erinaceus. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.
