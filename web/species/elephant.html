Elephant (ivory) in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 115 cases involving elephant (ivory) from public reports between 2024-01-15 and 2026-09-23, in 20 countries. 12 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Elephas maximus, Loxodonta africana. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.
