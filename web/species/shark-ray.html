Sharks, rays & sea cucumbers in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 58 cases involving sharks, rays & sea cucumbers from public reports between 2024-01-15 and 2026-09-26, in 14 countries. 3 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Selachimorpha, Batoidea, Holothuroidea, Hippocampus spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.
