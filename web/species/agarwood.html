Agarwood (oud) in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 5 cases involving agarwood (oud) from public reports between 2024-01-15 and 2026-09-24, in 3 countries. 1 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Aquilaria spp., Gyrinops spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.
