Monitor lizard in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 25 cases involving monitor lizard from public reports between 2024-01-15 and 2026-09-23, in 3 countries. 0 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Varanus bengalensis, Varanus flavescens, Varanus salvator. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.
